# Odyssey: twenty-work artwork journey

## Selection and use

This is an editorial shortlist for the launch exhibition, not a claim that these are objectively the twenty greatest works in the collection. It balances direct Odyssey recognition, emotional force, source resolution, geographic and narrative breadth, material variety and visible attribution.

Each work has one main exhibition placement. Card appearances retain their published artwork and are a separate art-to-game demonstration. The expandable index uses reference thumbnails; it is not another sequence of full-bleed backgrounds. No card designs or stored card crops are changed.

## Narrative itinerary

The overview precedes Troy. The route then moves through the Trojan-war background and the voyages, finally reaching the vineyard reunion in Odyssey XXIV. It follows the journey rather than Homer's non-linear narration. Mythical islands are not asserted modern geographical identifications.

## The invitation image

The participation section uses the face and upper body of Odysseus from John William Waterhouse's *Ulysses and the Sirens* (1891), the work currently assigned to Ordeal of the Sirens. The museum identification is [NGV collection record 4457](https://www.ngv.vic.gov.au/explore/collection/work/4457/). The upgraded reproduction is the 11,009 × 5,400 Google Art Project file via Wikimedia Commons, not a generated or upscaled substitute.

The detail is explicitly labelled. The full composition remains available through “See complete work”; the image source and museum record are both linked. No text, gradient, blur or signup control overlaps the face. The original card art, stored card crop and source image are left unchanged.

## Delivery and checks

The asset manifest records original dimensions, SHA-256 checksums, display derivatives and the crop's source rectangle. Same-origin reference thumbnails, 2,000-pixel display versions and up-to-3,600-pixel complete reproductions are created without upscaling. The face is cropped from the high-resolution source before resizing.

Metadata comes from the published artwork catalogue; the Waterhouse upgrade was checked against its Commons file and NGV record. This is not represented as a fresh scholarly audit of every catalogue attribution. Material identities and the distinction between direct Odyssey episodes, Trojan background and later reception remain explicit.

The browser verification checks twenty unique main placements, the twenty-entry index, carousel arrows/tabs/keyboard/wheel, complete-image viewing, an unobscured face tile, 390/768/1440-pixel layouts, and all 309 Studio-rendered candidates. Passing browser checks and production deployment are reported separately.
