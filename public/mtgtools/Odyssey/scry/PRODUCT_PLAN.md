# Odyssey public product plan

## Product truth

Odyssey is not a finished 309-card set. It is a working 309-card candidate file with **0 confirmed cards** as of 28 September 2026.

The public product should therefore behave like a development campaign, not a retrospective spoiler archive:

- prove that the set has enough depth to be worth finishing;
- make the mechanics and flavour immediately legible;
- let people inspect candidate cards while the open-preview window is active;
- recruit critique and playtest participation;
- fund the remaining design, testing, editing, tooling and presentation work without selling access to Wizards IP;
- eventually narrow the public surface from “everything” to curated previews as the internal candidate file becomes more controlled.

## Core pitch

**The war is over. The hard part is getting home.**

Odyssey is a custom Magic set built from Homer’s Odyssey and Theros-adjacent design language.

The stronger development hook is:

> What if a full fan set were finished in public, with its final card file shaped by structured community critique and playtesting?

Do not claim that it is literally “the first set developed by Reddit” unless that claim is researched and supportable. Use “developed with the community,” “built in public,” and “Reddit-driven development experiment.”

## Current funnel

### 1. Attraction
Large story / mechanic splashes should do most of the selling.

Each mechanic feature should show:
- an evocative one-sentence flavour promise;
- the colours currently carrying it;
- 2–3 representative cards;
- a background pulled from one of those cards;
- a route into all current candidates using that mechanic.

The user should understand why the mechanic belongs in the Odyssey before they understand the complete rules environment.

### 2. Proof
The candidate gallery is the proof-of-concept layer.

Current state:
- 309 working candidates;
- 0 confirmed cards;
- full candidate spoiler temporarily open.

Long-term:
- selected public previews remain open;
- the working candidate file can move into a tighter testing process;
- locked cards only become “confirmed” after design, templating, flavour, art-direction and playtesting review.

### 3. Participation
Community participation should be structured rather than an unrestricted vote.

Useful contribution channels:
- candidate critique;
- duplicate / weak-slot nominations;
- flavour failures;
- templating and rules issues;
- colour-pie issues;
- draft archetype and common-density feedback;
- playtest reports;
- replacement designs.

Public conversation can happen on Reddit, but the set owner retains final editorial responsibility.

### 4. Playtesting
Use small cohorts and logged results.

Suggested sequence:
1. sealed / draft skeleton testing;
2. archetype stress testing;
3. commons/uncommons pass;
4. bomb / removal / fixing pass;
5. whole-set draft;
6. final candidate cuts;
7. wording and presentation lock.

Supporters may opt into a tester pool, but payment should not be required to view fan content. Do not sell “access to the card file.”

## Defensible funding model

Wizards’ Fan Content Policy says fan content using Wizards IP must be free to access, while donations, ad revenue and sponsorship may subsidise the work so long as they do not interfere with community access.

Accordingly:

### Good funding directions
- Patreon / recurring donations supporting the development process;
- one-off donations;
- clearly labelled project sponsorship;
- reasonable sponsor / supporter acknowledgement;
- ad revenue;
- supporter usernames in a contributor ledger;
- per-candidate “development credit” acknowledgement attached to the public page, clearly described as sponsorship rather than ownership.

### Avoid promising
- paid access to the spoiler;
- paid access to card images or rules content;
- sale or licensing of the fan set;
- ownership of a card/design in exchange for payment;
- guaranteed paywalled playtest access;
- language implying Wizards endorses the project.

### Candidate-level sponsor concept
A card detail page may show:

**Development credit — [username]**

The credit means that the supporter subsidised work on the project during the phase in which this candidate was evaluated. It does **not** imply:
- ownership of the card;
- authorship of Wizards IP;
- that the candidate survives into the final file;
- exclusive access;
- Wizards affiliation.

If a candidate is cut, the historical development credit can remain in a contributor ledger.

Before accepting money specifically tied to card-face placement, get legal review. Prefer credits adjacent to the card rather than inserted into the Magic-style game frame.

## Preview lifecycle

### Stage A — current: open proof of concept
- all 309 candidates visible;
- giant “0 locked” messaging;
- mechanics presented as flavour experiences;
- solicit critique;
- social preview generator available.

### Stage B — structured development
- public site emphasises mechanic previews and selected cards;
- working candidate file becomes a controlled test surface;
- publish change logs and design decisions;
- community feedback becomes issue-based / round-based rather than continuous.

### Stage C — candidate shortlist
- cards begin receiving explicit confidence / lock status;
- only reviewed candidates become public “spoilers”;
- cut cards remain available as a development archive if useful.

### Stage D — final release
- locked free set;
- final set story / mechanics pages;
- complete gallery;
- release article / making-of;
- playtest and contributor credits.

## Visual direction

### Card rendering
The public renderer must:
- scale all card typography with card width;
- reduce font size further for long names, type lines and rules boxes;
- render mana as visual pips rather than raw text;
- use the same renderer in gallery, mechanic splashes, card detail and social-preview live composition;
- preserve legibility down to social-preview thumbnail size.

### Promo identity
Current logo is provisional.

Directions worth exploring:
- an “O” built from a horizon / returning wake;
- oar / ship-prow geometry;
- Ithaca / homecoming rather than generic Greek-key decoration;
- restrained archaic lettering with modern Magic-set marketing proportions;
- a small set mark that can survive at card-size, separate from the large ODYSSEY marketing lockup.

Avoid copying Wizards or Magic logos.

### Social system
Promo builder:
- 1–5 selected cards;
- selected card supplies the background image;
- Gaussian-blurred full-bleed artwork;
- mirrored lower field to extend the image naturally;
- square, landscape, portrait and story outputs;
- Odyssey promo lockup;
- canonical URL;
- eventual presets for mechanic reveal, character reveal, cycle reveal, “five cards to understand X,” and community-development update.

## Definition of a confirmed card

A candidate should not count as confirmed until it passes:
1. mechanical role;
2. colour-pie role;
3. Limited environment need;
4. duplication check;
5. flavour / story hook;
6. templating;
7. power-band testing;
8. art-direction suitability;
9. final editorial review.

Until that exists in the data model, the public count remains **0 confirmed**.
