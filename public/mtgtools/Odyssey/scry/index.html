The story

An epic built as a playable world.

Odysseus has survived Troy, but the route back to Ithaca becomes its own war. The set follows the crew through divine grudges, impossible choices, seduction, shipwreck, monsters and strange islands, while Penelope and Telemachus fight a quieter battle at home.

01

Myth as game structure

Major episodes become cards, cycles and mechanical through-lines rather than one-off references.

02

Theros DNA

Enchantments, devotion, sagas and mythic archetypes create a familiar Magic vocabulary for an ancient story.

03

Built to draft

The set is designed as a real Limited environment, with color identities, signposts, commons and build-around themes.

The experiment

What if the final set were developed with the community?

The design skeleton is real. The final choices are deliberately not. The next phase is about turning a large working file into a defensible final set through structured critique, playtesting, recasting and public design discussion.

01

Open development

Use Reddit and public design notes to pressure-test flavour, templating, cycles, colour balance and individual card candidates.

02

Playtest cohorts

Run small, trackable draft and gameplay rounds. Supporters can opt into the tester pool, but the fan content itself stays freely viewable.

03

Fund the finishing work

Patreon, donations and clearly labelled sponsorship can subsidise illustration research, testing, tooling and editing without turning the spoiler into paid access.

04

Leave your mark

Contributor and sponsor usernames can be acknowledged in a restrained credit ledger or allocated supporter space attached to the free project—recognition, not ownership of the card.
