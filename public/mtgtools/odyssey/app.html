Crop direction

Drag with one finger to move the artwork. Pinch with two fingers to zoom naturally on mobile. Desktop: drag to pan and use the mouse wheel / trackpad to zoom. The full image remains behind the frame. Move it freely, including beyond the frame edges. Use the full-image view to choose any part of the artwork.

Artwork metadata

Renderer route

Legendary type lines automatically receive the crown title frame. Adventure, Prepare and Battle routes automatically split the source rules into the secondary inset. Saga and Vehicle routes receive layout-specific furniture; Transform keeps the front-face frame and presents the back face in a dedicated inset while retaining the same editable data model.

Production model

Procedural globally, directed locally
Global frame changes can be applied across every card in the active dataset. This card retains only its stored overrides: crop, art choice, display alias and any manual text/layout changes.
