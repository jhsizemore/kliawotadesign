# Books V–VIII editorial audit

**Date:** 23 September 2026

Scope: 51 cards grounded primarily in Odyssey V–VIII.

## Outcomes

- 9 new exact Butler quotation candidates.
- 35 new source-led adaptation candidates.
- 3 deliberate no-flavour decisions (the three Island basics).
- Existing exact candidates for Nausicaa, Calypso, Euryalus and Song of the Wooden Horse retained.

## Making-of distinctions

- **Calypso:** care and coercion remain simultaneously visible; Refuge or Captivity is not framed as freely chosen romance.
- **Nausicaa:** courage is hospitality before identity is known.
- **Arete:** authority comes through discernment and social judgement.
- **Demodocus:** accuracy of song exposes concealed emotion and identity.
- **Phaeacian craft:** supernatural ships are textual; individual shipwrights, smiths and several workshop scenes are explicit extensions.
- **Sea craft:** Odysseus's raft-building is direct Book V action; named specialist sailors and craft workers are extensions.
- **Landscape cards:** invented coves/isles/grounds use source imagery without pretending the names occur in Homer.
- **Ogygia:** seven years are textual; altered time is metaphor, not literal time travel.

Exact source links and candidate wording are stored in the editorial register and flavour-text audit.
