# Hybrid Saga audit

**Date:** 23 September 2026  
**Status:** Mechanical colour-pie gate resolved in isolated candidate; gameplay rates not certified.

## Rule

Hybrid mana is an **or**, not an **and**. Each hybrid design must be a card either component colour could receive by itself.

- Hybrid design history: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/the-history-of-hybrid-part-1
- Mechanical colour pie: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/mechanical-color-pie-2021

## Cycle

| Pair | Card | Result | Resolution |
|---|---|---|---|
| UR | ODY-097 **Song of Hephaestus's Net** | Pass + naming repair | Scry is shared; Treasure is red-primary/blue-tertiary; U and R both support spells and artifacts. Rename makes the actual Demodocus performance visible. |
| BG | ODY-162 **Song of the Faithless Crew** | Revised | Keep mill three. Chapter II becomes “Create a Food token and a Treasure token.” Chapter III keeps creature recursion. |
| RW | ODY-163 **Song of Achilles** | Pass | Red and white both support creature tokens, +N/+N combat tricks, first strike and double strike. |
| WB | ODY-232 **Lament at the Blood-Trench** | Pass | Spirit/token production, graveyard interaction, life gain and small-creature reanimation sit in white-black overlap. |
| GU | ODY-245 **Song of the Faithful Queen** | Revised | Chapter III now untaps creatures and/or lands, then grants hexproof; green should not get unrestricted arbitrary-permanent untapping. |

## Edge-case evidence

- **Green self-mill / investigate:** *Flotsam* is a printed {G/U} hybrid instant: “Mill three cards. Investigate.”  
  https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/murders-at-karlov-manor-release-notes
- **Black-green Food:** *Wilds of Eldraine* deliberately limited its Food draft theme to black and green.  
  https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/making-magic/wilds-story-part-2
- **Treasure:** Mechanical Color Pie 2021 lists Treasure as primary red, secondary black/green and tertiary blue.
- **RW pump:** the same reference permits +N/+N spells in white and red and double strike in both.
- **GU untap:** blue may untap arbitrary permanents; green specifically gets untapping creatures and lands, defining the overlap used here.

## Song / performance status

- **ODY-097:** direct source-text performance. In *Odyssey* VIII, Demodocus sings the affair of Ares and Aphrodite and Hephaestus's trap.  
  https://classics.mit.edu/Homer/odyssey.8.viii.html
- **ODY-162:** invented/composite cautionary song about the crew's repeated failures.
- **ODY-163:** modern reception, currently tied to *The Song of Achilles*.
- **ODY-232:** invented/adaptive lament of the underworld episode.
- **ODY-245:** invented/adaptive song interpreting Penelope's endurance.

The cycle shares a **performed/retold song frame**, not a claim that all five songs literally occur in Homer.

## Remaining test questions

1. Is ODY-162 too value-dense at {2}{B/G} when chapter II no longer asks for a sacrifice?
2. Does ODY-245's narrower final chapter still feel worth waiting for in both mono-green and mono-blue decks?
3. Does the ODY-097 rename, alongside its art, make the song-performance layer legible without reminder prose?
4. Do the five cards read as one interpretive cycle in draft despite deliberately different source statuses?
