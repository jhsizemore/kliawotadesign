# Cross-book Odyssey synthesis audit

**Date:** 23 September 2026

Resolved concepts and portrayals that cross the strict book clusters.

- nostos — belonging restored after arrival
- xenia — obligation before identity
- metis — timing, concealment and leverage
- moira — constraint without removal of responsibility
- recognition — evidence reaching certainty
- hidden names — information control
- Zeus/social order — witness and sanction rather than automatic rescue
- whole-voyage mythics — accumulation of failed routes, transformed arrivals and crew labour

Deferred: ODY-172 to Homeric similes; ODY-180 to Trojan/pre-Odyssey.
