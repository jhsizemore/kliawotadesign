# Odyssey Limited collation model

**ID:** odyssey-core-fin-playbooster-v1  
**Locked:** 23 September 2026  
**Purpose:** analytical Draft/Sealed testing only; not a manufactured-product claim.

## Why this is the correct chassis

Wizards' published FINAL FANTASY set structure is 309 cards: 80 ordinary commons, 10 common lands, 16 basics, 109 uncommons, 74 rares and 20 mythic rares. Odyssey's current 309-card candidate matches that split exactly when its 106 common-rarity records are decomposed into 80 ordinary commons + 10 common dual lands + 16 basics.

FIN Play Boosters contain 14 game cards: 6–7 commons, 3 uncommons, 1 wildcard, 1 rare/mythic, 1 foil wildcard and 1 land. One third of FIN packs replace a common with a Through the Ages bonus-sheet card.

Official source: https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/collecting-final-fantasy

If Odyssey later receives an external bonus sheet that replaces one dedicated common in one third of packs, the expected ordinary-common count becomes **7.4182 per pack**. That is only 0.3333 fewer and does not reverse the current common-density findings.

## Design reading

**Infrastructure:** Food, Survival, enchantments, self-mill and Omens are visible enough at common to be read during a draft.

**Accents:** Escape and Constellation each have only one common card. They can add texture, but a colour pair must not require them to function.

**Supporting mechanics:** Gift and Foretell have two genuine common cards each. Their colour-pair themes need broader surrounding actions rather than expecting the named mechanic itself to appear repeatedly.

**Density watch:** GU has only two common Manifest Fate sources in its own colours. The set has three total. This is not automatically a failure because an eight-player table exposes roughly seven Manifest commons before picks, but it is thin enough that the next test must model who can actually acquire them.

**Recognition:** WU has only two direct common blink/return cards, but its signposts also read face-up/exile events. Test the union of those enablers rather than treating blink count as the archetype's entire as-fan.

## What this model does not prove

It does not model print collation correlations, duplicate protection, pick order, colour competition, deck construction, mana availability, sequencing or games. Those remain separate gates.
