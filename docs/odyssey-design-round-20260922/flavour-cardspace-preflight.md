## Policy

- Do not delete source/flavour research from the editorial register for space.
- A printed-card omission is a layout decision only; the making-of record retains the candidate text.
- Direct quotations are not privileged over rules legibility.
- Special frames must be checked in their actual Saga/Adventure/Prepare/Transform/Vehicle architecture.
- Any card that Studio labels `compressed` or `overflow` returns to this queue for shortening or omission.
