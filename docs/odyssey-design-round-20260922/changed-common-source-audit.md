# Changed-common source audit

**Date:** 23 September 2026  
**Scope:** the final ten of 24 changed commons that still lacked passage-level review.  
**Result:** all 24 changed commons now have either a checked passage or an explicit extension classification.

| Slot | Card | Classification | Source finding |
|---|---|---|---|
| 044 | Row in Unison | Direct + mechanical abstraction | Odyssey XII: Odysseus orders the crew to “row on with might and main” and separately commands the coxswain. Delayed untap is a gameplay abstraction. |
| 064 | Black-Hulled Ship | Direct composite + abstraction | Odyssey IX and XII show boarding, taking oar positions, ship handling and dangerous passage. Recovering one crew member after attack is mechanical. |
| 074 | Set Bread Before the Guest | Direct | Odyssey VII seats Odysseus and serves bread and household food before further questioning. |
| 087 | Seabird Lookout | Explicit extension | Odyssey V uses cormorant and sea-gull imagery around sea travel. It does **not** narrate a mortal sailor reading seabirds to choose a course. |
| 103 | Libation Attendant | Direct | Odyssey III shows a cup handed for prayer and drink-offering, then passed onward. |
| 108 | Household Cupbearer | Direct | Odyssey III explicitly has pages fill mixing-bowls with wine and water and hand it around. |
| 112 | Dolphins Beside the Bow | Explicit extension | Odyssey XII explicitly names dolphins among Scylla's marine prey. It does **not** narrate dolphins running beside Odysseus's bow. |
| 115 | Depth-Sounding Sailor | Historical seamanship extension | Odyssey IX supplies crew/oar/harbour work, not depth sounding. Ancient Mediterranean sounding practice is externally attested; the card must not present it as a Homeric episode. |
| 123 | Shade of the Waiting House | Explicit fantasy extension | Odyssey XXIV opens with the slain suitors as named ghosts in Hades. A shade escaping bodily back into play is deliberate fantasy. |
| 142 | Pour to the Dead | Direct + symbolic mechanics | Odyssey XI directly gives the drink-offering to the dead and ghosts approaching the trench. Battlefield reanimation is symbolic. |
