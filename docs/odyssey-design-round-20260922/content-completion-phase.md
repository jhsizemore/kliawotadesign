# Odyssey content-completion phase

**Started:** 23 September 2026  
**Scope:** all 309 physical cards.

## Definition of done

A card leaves this phase only when all three content systems agree:

1. **MTG references** — printed comparison/precedent set is current for the final rules.
2. **Making-of** — action/sequence, emotion, defining attribute, gameplay hooks and portrayal distinction are specific and source-classified.
3. **Flavour text** — a verified quotation candidate with attribution/provenance, or an explicit editorial decision that the card should carry no direct quotation/flavour text.

A quotation is not accepted merely because it sounds good. A source label is not accepted merely because it points to the right book. Composite and invented material stays labelled as such.

## Narrative work order

Work by source cluster so repeated characters/scenes are checked together:

1. Books I–IV — Telemachy
2. Books V–VIII — Calypso, Scheria, Phaeacians
3. Books IX–XII — wanderings and crew failures
4. Books XIII–XVI — return, disguise, reconnaissance
5. Books XVII–XX — palace recognition, suitors, omens
6. Books XXI–XXIV — bow, slaughter, reunions, peace
7. Trojan/pre-Odyssey material
8. Post-Odyssey/later ancient tradition
9. Modern reception and adaptations

At each cluster: verify the making brief first, then choose quotation candidates, then check repeated quotations and card-space risk. Do not wait for gameplay unless the wording itself depends on a mechanical redesign.

## Flavour outcome types

A completed flavour **outcome** may be one of three things:

- **Verified quotation candidate** — exact wording checked against a named source/translation.
- **Source-led adaptation candidate** — original Odyssey Studio wording grounded in a checked passage or explicitly labelled extension; never attributed to Homer.
- **No flavour selected** — deliberate layout/editorial decision, such as a basic land treatment.

“No direct quote selected” by itself is now an intermediate classification, not a completed flavour outcome. Composite cards should normally receive a source-led adaptation unless silence is the better layout choice.
