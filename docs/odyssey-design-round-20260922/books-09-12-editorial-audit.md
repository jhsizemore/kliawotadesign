## Core emotional distinctions
- Cyclops: guest-right violated; cunning wins, then pride reopens danger.
- Lotus-Eaters: relief erases purpose rather than inflicting pain.
- Aeolus: a precise gift fails because trust collapses after the giver is absent.
- Circe: identity is physically rewritten; resistance depends on preparation.
- Hades: knowledge requires ritual, fear, grief and obligations to the dead.
- Sirens: knowledge is attractive enough to become lethal.
- Scylla/Charybdis: survival means accepting a loss that leaves a voyage afterward.
- Helios: hunger converts taboo into rationalised short-term survival, then consequence.
- Crew cards: ordinary coordination and fatigue are the connective tissue between the famous episodes.
